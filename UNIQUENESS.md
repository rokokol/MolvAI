# Uniqueness report

The full report is [docs/uniqueness.md](docs/uniqueness.md): the analogues we studied, what we took from them, what we wrote ourselves, and how the key steps differ

In short, we took exactly one file: `scripts/t.sh` from [rokokol/tests-skill](https://github.com/rokokol/tests-skill) under MIT, with the source named in the header. We wrote the audio capture, the recognition and post-processing pipeline, the Wayland paste chain, the JSONL journal, the statistics, the IPC, the daemon and the GUI for MolvAI at the hackathon. We studied the open analogues (Wispr Flow, Handy, Whispering, nerd-dictation, Talon) as a guide to behaviour, and we did not copy their code into the repository
